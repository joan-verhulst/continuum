# Landing page (marketing)

The product is **Larder**, the back office for food wholesalers. This is its public landing page.

**Top bar**: the name "Larder"; links Product, Pricing, Customers, Docs; "Sign in" and "Start free trial".

**Hero**: headline "The back office for food wholesalers." Line: "Orders, stock and invoices in one place, so
your team spends the morning on customers, not spreadsheets." Actions: "Start free trial" (main), "Book a demo".
Under it, a product screenshot (no network: a placeholder).

**Logos**: "Trusted by 400 wholesalers": De Groene Keuken, Hotel Meridiaan, Bakkerij Vos, Brasserie Noord,
Kantine Westpoort.

**Three features**:
1. Orders from every channel: "Wholesale, web shop and phone orders land in one list."
2. Stock that tells you: "Low-stock alerts before a customer finds out."
3. Invoices that send themselves: "Every delivery invoiced, every overdue one chased."

**Pricing** (per month): Starter €49 (1 warehouse, 3 users), Growth €149 (3 warehouses, 15 users, most
popular), Scale €399 (unlimited warehouses and users, SSO). Each with "Start free trial".
