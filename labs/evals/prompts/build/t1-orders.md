# Orders (an index)

The product is **Larder**, the back office of a food wholesaler. This is its list of orders.

**Sidebar**: the product name "Larder". Places: Overview, Orders (the current page), Customers, Products,
Invoices, Reports. At the foot: Settings, and the signed-in account "Mira Jansen", mira@larder.co.

**Page**: title "Orders", line "Every order from every channel, newest first."

**Actions**: one primary, "New order". One secondary, "Export CSV".

**Views** of the list, with their counts: All (128), Open (23), Shipped (91), Cancelled (14). "All" is selected.

**Narrowing**: a search ("Search orders"), and filters by Status, Channel and Date. One filter is applied:
Channel is Wholesale.

**Columns**: Order, Customer, Date, Channel, Items, Total, Status. Rows can be selected together (a checkbox per
row) and each row has a menu (Duplicate, Cancel order).
